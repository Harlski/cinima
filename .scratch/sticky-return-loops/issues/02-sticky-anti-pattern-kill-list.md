## Question

Which common “retention” patterns are **explicitly out** for NimCharts sticky exploration — even if they raise open rates — because they fight simplicity or the taste-overlap job?

Examples to decide include: daily streaks, gamified points, empty algorithmic “For You” noise, obligation notifications, watch-diary completeness pressure, infinite social feeds, leaderboards. Produce a short kill list with one-line reasons, to filter later candidate tickets.

## Answer

Nine retention patterns are **vetoed** for sticky candidates (streaks with loss/guilt, points/XP/badges, opaque For You fill, obligation nags, personal watch logging, infinite feed firehose, leaderboards, pay-to-see social, artificial FOMO timers), each with a one-line reason. Soft carve-outs (weekly non-loss rhythm cues, genuine overlap ranking, bounded feeds, paid actions, real premiere windows) stay eligible to argue later.
