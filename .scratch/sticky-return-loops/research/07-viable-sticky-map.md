# Two-bucket viable sticky ideas map

**Destination artifact for sticky-return-loops.** No build commitment. No ship-priority ranking.

Assembled after: inventory `01`, kill list `02`, deepen `03`, personal `04`, social `05`, surface filter `06`.

**Viable bar:** Fits **taste-overlap** primary job AND stays **simple** for a weekly-ish returner. Cadence: a few times a week when something meaningful changed. Signals: in-app only.

**Surface budget used:** 0 of 1 new surfaces (all ideas fit Discover / Activity / Me / Title).

---

## 1. Deepen existing

| Name | Why it clears the viable bar |
|---|---|
| Following “new since last visit” (bounded) | Makes peer favorite/unlock change detectable without nags or infinite scroll. |
| For you: new / rose-in-overlap marks | Shows taste-graph motion on the primary discovery surface. |
| Activity personal/followed slice (finite) | Social change you care about on an existing tab; fixes global firehose mismatch. |
| Honest thin-graph continuity on Discover | Truthful empty/partial states that point at densifying follows/overlaps — no fake fill. |
| Title: recent overlap-or-follow peer motion | Taste signal at the decision moment without pay-to-see. |

## 2. Candidate net-new

| Name | Why it clears the viable bar |
|---|---|
| Weekly favorites curation / taste-refresh (personal) | Favorites are the taste vector; non-loss weekly-or-looser prune/add renews overlap without a watch diary. |
| Overlap people-to-follow (social) | Densifies the graph so Following / For you can actually move a few times a week. |
| Overlap-set taste moves (social) | Reopen reason when *taste-peers* (not only followees) change favorites — primary job, bounded digest. |

---

## Not on the viable map (summary)

Killed patterns (02): daily/loss streaks, points/XP/badges, opaque For You fill, obligation nags, personal watch diary, infinite feed firehose, leaderboards, pay-to-see social, artificial FOMO.

Also parked/rejected across 03–05: cold-start as weekly loop, Search sticky, unlock-TTL as sticky, heatmap-as-reopen, public-profile-as-retention, NIM-sink cadence, stranger Activity, chat/threads.
