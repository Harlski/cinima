# Sticky anti-pattern kill list

Question: Which common retention patterns are **explicitly out** for NimCharts sticky exploration — even if they raise open rates — because they fight simplicity or the taste-overlap job?

**How to use:** Later candidate tickets (deepen / personal-ritual / social-change) must not smuggle these patterns. Soft carve-outs below are allowed to argue; they are **not** kills.

**Scope:** Retention-pattern vetoes for sticky candidates only. Map **Out of scope** (push/email/OS alerts, full diary product, changing primary job, etc.) stays locked separately — this list does not re-litigate those.

**Viable bar reminder:** fits taste-overlap primary job AND stays simple for a weekly-ish returner. Cadence target: a few times a week when something meaningful changed; in-app only.

---

## Kill list

| # | Pattern (killed) | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Daily / loss-guilt streaks** (open daily to keep a flame; broken-streak shame; freeze tokens) | Fights few-times-a-week change cadence; guilt open rates are not the taste-overlap job. |
| 2 | **Gamified points / XP / badges** as a reopen reason | Parallel meta-game job; taste discovery is not a score grind. |
| 3 | **Opaque algorithmic “For You” fill** (engagement ranking; look-busy popular padding pretending to be personalization) | Fakes taste overlap; opaque engagement bait fights honesty and the primary job. |
| 4 | **Obligation nags** (come-back / missing-out / “you haven’t visited” theater), including in-app | Manufactures urgency without taste change. Outside-Pay push/email already banned on the map. |
| 5 | **Personal watch logging / diary** as a sticky loop (including light “seen” archives) | Favorites + unlocks are enough; personal watch log pulls toward a media-tracker job. Full diary product is also map Out of scope. |
| 6 | **Infinite social scroll firehose** (endless never-caught-up Activity/Following as the sticky habit) | Feed addiction and network noise fight simplicity; reopen should not require endless scrolling. |
| 7 | **Leaderboards / competitive rank contests** (most unlocks, tippers, weekly champions, etc.) | Prestige reopen, not discovery. Overlap ranking of titles/peers for taste ≠ a prestige scoreboard. |
| 8 | **Pay-to-see social** (gate who favorited / taste overlap / social change behind payment) | Rents the social graph; seeing taste signal should stay free. Paid *actions* (unlock catalog reveal, post comment, tip) remain product economics. |
| 9 | **Artificial FOMO scarcity timers** (flash deals, fake “only 2h left,” manufactured limited drops for opens) | Fake urgency for reopen; not meaningful taste change. |

---

## Explicitly not killed (carve-outs)

These may still appear in later candidate tickets; they must still clear the viable bar and not smuggle a kill above.

| Carve-out | Bound |
|---|---|
| Non-loss rhythm cues | Weekly-or-looser; no daily counter that punishes a quiet day; no loss/shame framing. |
| Genuine overlap ranking | Peer/taste-overlap Discover stays on-job; do not replace with opaque engagement fill. |
| Popular fallback when the graph is empty | Allowed if it does not *pretend* to be overlap personalization. |
| Change-true chrome | “New since last visit” when peers actually acted is not an obligation nag by itself (argue in deepen tickets). |
| Bounded / finite feeds | Finite “what changed” or recent lists OK to deepen; kill is endlessness as the habit. |
| Paid actions | Unlock ratings/heatmaps, paid comments, tips — not pay-to-*see* who shares taste. |
| Real taste time windows | e.g. premiere week / real calendar moments — not countdown theater. |
| Taste-native product numbers | `sharedCount`, follower counts, heat-map cells as discovery signals — not XP/points as a meta currency (points/XP/badges still killed). |
