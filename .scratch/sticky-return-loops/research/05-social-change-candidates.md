# Social-change candidates (draft)

Question: Which **social-change** sticky ideas (beyond what’s already in the product) would give a reason to reopen a few times a week when other people’s taste changed — while staying in-app and simple? Which fail the viable bar or the kill list?

**Scope:** Net-new *social* loops (or explicit rejects). Continuity chrome on Following / For you / Activity / Title is deepen (`03` D1–D5) — do not duplicate here. Kill list `02` binding. In-app only.

**Viable bar:** Fits taste-overlap primary job AND stays simple for a weekly-ish returner.

**Auth:** Remaining grill branches use recommended answers (user direction for parallel close-out).

---

## Candidates — for net-new bucket

| # | Idea | Why it might clear the viable bar |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Overlap people-to-follow** (suggest wallets that share favorites; follow densifies weekly Following/For you motion) | Social graph hygiene that serves taste discovery; unlocks D1/D3 change volume without leaving the job. |
| S2 | **Overlap-set taste moves** (when people who share taste — not only followees — favorite something new / surface a new mutual title) | Catch-all social change for the primary job when you haven’t followed everyone yet; bounded digest, not a strangers firehose. |

---

## Rejects

| Idea | Why-not |
|---|---|
| Infinite global Activity / stranger firehose as sticky | Kill #6; weak personal relevance (inventory Activity). |
| Leaderboards / top tippers / unlock champs | Kill #7. |
| Pay-to-see who favorited / who overlaps | Kill #8. |
| Obligation “someone might have posted” nags with no real change | Kill #4. |
| Opaque engagement-ranked social For You | Kill #3. |
| Artificial scarcity / limited social drops | Kill #9. |
| Tips/thanks volume as the reopen loop | Sparse + payment friction; inbound thanks still weak without personal filter (partly covered if D3 lives). |
| Live chat / threads / stories | Complexity spike; leaves taste-overlap mini-app job. |
| Share / public-profile loops as *retention* | Leave as acquisition unless proven otherwise (gap #4); not in-app Pay reopen. |

---

## Boundary notes

- **S2 vs D1:** D1 = followed only. S2 = overlap peers even if not followed — new loop, can sit as a Discover section or Activity slice without a new tab.
- **S1 vs D4:** D4 is honest empty state; S1 is the affirmative people-discovery action that densifies. Complementary.
- **Bounded:** Both must ship as finite digests / short lists (kill #6 carve-out), never endless scroll theater.
- **Density risk:** S1 is the structural fix for gaps #3/#9; S2 still needs *some* co-favorite peers to move.
