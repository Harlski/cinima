# Guided tour +10 NIM copy is the Join grant, not a second Send

The tour offer and wrap screen dangle +10 NIM so people finish the walkthrough instead of skipping. That NIM is the existing Join grant (one per wallet at sign-in). Completing does not Send another 10 NIM, and replay does not either. A second Send would be farmable from Me and would double the welcome cost; withholding the Join grant until complete would reverse ADR 0011 and 0012.
